# Mission T-001 — VENGA41 — "Logistics air bridge to Solenzara"

## Introduction

Type: AIRLIFT  
Subtype: STRATEGIC / CARGO  
Aircraft: A400M Atlas  
Callsign: VENGA41  
Departure: BA 123 Orléans-Bricy (LFOJ) — 47.987778, 1.760556  
Destination: BA 126 Solenzara (LFKS) — 41.926389, 9.405278

## Summary

Fly a heavy, high-priority load from Orléans-Bricy to restore the capability of a detachment deployed at Solenzara. The flight combines load preparation, centre-of-gravity management, a long-range transit and arrival at an active military base. The logistics unit reports that the two generators are "ready on a single pallet"; two separate pallets are eventually presented for loading. The mission remains planned for a sequenced unload so that electrical power is available first.

## Detailed description

The crew prepares an A400M departing from BA 123 Orléans-Bricy. The main load consists of two palletised generators, telecommunications modules and consumables, for a total planned mass of 12,800 kg. Before closing up, the pallet distribution must be checked to keep a centre of gravity compatible with the flight and with the unloading order requested on arrival.

The transit reaches Corsica via several geographic checkpoints, with weather monitoring over the Massif Central, the Rhône valley corridor and then the sea crossing. Endurance must include a reserve suited to possible holding or a diversion, as the destination is a military base whose activity may require sequencing.

On arrival, the approach into Solenzara is coordinated with the reception team at RDV-1, located on the airfield. Taxiing to the cargo apron must maintain separation from other movements. Unloading is carried out with engines shut down, starting with the generators, then the telecommunications modules.

The mission is successful if all the freight arrives undamaged, within the set window, with a consistent load file and unloading in the planned order. A secondary objective is to prepare a realistic diversion option before departure and to keep enough fuel to fly it if access to Solenzara is delayed.

## Primary objective

Deliver the 12,800 kg of priority freight to Solenzara within the planned logistics window, undamaged and with a compliant centre of gravity.

## Secondary objectives

- Meet an arrival window of ±15 minutes around the planned time.
- Prepare an alternate airfield before departure and keep the associated reserve.
- Unload the generators before the telecommunications modules.

## Risks to consider

- **En-route weather and sea crossing** — consequence: A front or local convection may increase flight time or force a detour. Mitigation: Update weather and fuel before departure; keep a routing and diversion margin.
- **Military traffic at Solenzara** — consequence: An arrival slot may be delayed by local activity. Mitigation: Plan holding fuel and early radio coordination.
- **Heavy palletised load** — consequence: Poor distribution may degrade the centre of gravity and floor loading. Mitigation: Check mass, pallet positions, tie-down and load sheet before closing up.
