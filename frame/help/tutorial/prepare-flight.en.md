# Prepare a flight

*Five minutes, in the order of the five steps of Fuel: from an empty screen to a flight checked on the map, with its
fuel, ready to fly and to report.*

**Before you start.** Nothing is required. Your trigram in ⚙ Settings › Identity lets the report recognise you later;
with a SimBrief ID, Fuel can also take your last plan in one go.

## 1. Flight

![The Flight step: airfields, aircraft and level](img/prepare-flight-1.en.png)

**Where to.** Open Fuel from the menu (☰). Type the departure, the arrival and the alternate airfield, four letters
each: LFBO, LFRN, LFRS.

**With what.** Touch the magnifying glass and type your aircraft: a registration, a callsign or a type, such as TBM9.
Choose it in the list. The result appears at once at the bottom of the screen and follows every change.

**How high.** Type a level in “Level (FL)”, for example 280; you will choose the best one at step 4. Then “Next ·
Route”.

## 2. Route

![The map in Edit mode: the route points get a circle](img/prepare-flight-2.en.png)

**Type the route.** In “Route”, write the points and airways as in a flight plan: LFBO TOU UN460 NTS LFRN. Leave the
field: the route is tidied up, and the distance shows just below.

**Check it on the map.** “Show the map” opens Map on your flight. Touch “Edit”: drag a point to move it, touch a beacon
to add it, then “Done”. Fuel follows each change.

**Come back.** “‹ Fuel” at the top brings you back to the Route step. Then “Next · Weather”.

## 3. Weather

![The Weather step: the real wind along the route](img/prepare-flight-3.en.png)

**The airfields' weather.** Online, it is read by itself: below the wind, each airfield of the trip gives its
category, the wind on its runway, the ceiling and the visibility. “Read the aerodromes’ weather” reads it again.

**The real wind.** Tick “Real weather: wind along the route”: the wind fields take the forecast wind at your level.

**The temperature.** “Use ISA …” takes the day's temperature from the departure airfield. Then “Next · Quote”.

## 4. Quote

![The level decision: fuel and time for each level](img/prepare-flight-4.en.png)

**Choose the level.** The drawing gives the fuel (solid line) and the time (dashes) for each level; the green dot marks
the most economical one. Touch “Use FL…”, or touch the curve.

**Check the ceiling.** In great heat or from a high airfield, unfold “Density altitude”: it says at which altitude the
aircraft reaches its ceiling that day.

**Read the result.** The bar at the bottom gives the block fuel, the trip fuel, the flight time and where the descent
starts. Then “Next · Departure”.

## 5. Departure

![The Departure step: the summary and “Start the flight”](img/prepare-flight-5.en.png)

**Check the summary.** Aircraft, route, level, wind and block fuel, in one card.

**Start the flight.** “Start the flight” opens the map on your flight and keeps it for the report. After landing,
follow [Write a report](#help?t=write-report).
