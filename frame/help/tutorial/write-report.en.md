# Write a report

*After the flight, five minutes: from the flight you started, or your last flight on IVAO, to the report saved,
recorded on the airline website and found again in My flights.*

**Before you start.** Your trigram in ⚙ Settings › Identity. To take your flight from the network, your IVAO (or
VATSIM) ID there too, and the device online.

## 1. Flight

![The report offers the flight you started](img/write-report-1.en.png)

**Take your flight.** Open Report from the menu (☰). A card at the top offers your flight: “Flight started at …” for
the one started in Fuel, or “Last flight on IVAO” from the network. Touch “Use”.

**Fill in the rest.** The card says in one line what it takes: aircraft, airfields, engine start time and departure
fuel from Fuel, or all the times from the network. A small label above each field says where it comes from. Add what
is missing: registration, callsign, the other times. Then “Next →”.

## 2. Mission

![The Mission step: family and series](img/write-report-2.en.png)

**Choose the family.** The kind of mission — airlift, firefighting, medical… — then its series. The last family you
used is offered first.

**Add the details.** The blocks below follow the family. Fill in what you know: nothing is mandatory. Then “Next →”.

## 3. Result

![The Result step: outcome and crew](img/write-report-3.en.png)

**The outcome.** Choose it, and write your observations if you have any.

**The crew and the fuel.** You are already in the crew, as aircraft commander; “+ Member” adds the others. Lower down,
check the fuel at departure and give the fuel at landing.

## 4. Save and record on the website

![What is still missing, and the two buttons](img/write-report-4.en.png)

**What is missing.** A line lists the fields still empty. It never blocks you: you can save now and complete later.

**Save.** “Save” keeps the report and gives it its number.

**Record on the website.** “Save and record on the website” saves and opens the airline website's form with your
flight. Log in if needed, run the “→ Vol” bookmark (the “On the airline website” fold says how to add it once), check,
then submit on the website.

## 5. My flights

![My flights: the new flight at the top](img/write-report-5.en.png)

**Find it again.** Open My flights from the menu: your flight is at the top, with its block time. A flight still
waiting for its report has an orange dot: touch it, then “Write the report”.

**To go further.** The [Report](#help?m=report) and [My flights](#help?m=flightlog) chapters of the manual.
