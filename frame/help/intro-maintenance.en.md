# Maintenance — manual

The Maintenance app is for the airline's administrator: the data it publishes for the pilots (Data module), the fleet and
its performance sheets (Aircraft module), and the suite's administration (Settings, Administration card). It opens at the
Full level. With the workstation's launcher it writes; without a launcher, or from a paired device, it is read only.

## First start

- **The entries.** On a phone, the bottom bar gives Data, Administration and Help: the header does not repeat them. On a
  screen they are the entries of the left column, with the workstation's service pages (pilots' tokens, devices, V2
  maintenance page). The application's name, top left, opens the list of applications: one tap goes to Pilot.
- **The help.** It opens at the chapter of the screen shown; this manual covers Maintenance only, Pilot's stays in Pilot.
- **The app's status.** The dot on the Administration entry (green: something new; orange: something needs your
  attention) leads to the “Status” block at the top of Settings.
