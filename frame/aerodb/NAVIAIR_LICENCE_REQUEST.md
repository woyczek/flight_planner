# Naviair (Denmark, Greenland, the Faroe Islands): licence request for reading the AIP data, ready to send (10 November 2026)

*For the owner to send. The session sends nothing and writes no personal data. Draft only; nothing from Naviair is stored in the repository.*

## 1. What was read, and why the reader is not written

- **The terms**, read on 10 November 2026 in the GEN 0.1 Preface of the Danish AIP (`aim.naviair.dk/media/files/…/EK_GEN_0_1_en.pdf`, section 6 « Copyright »):
  « As certain information in the publications are the property of the Danish CAA and/or third parties, no part may be reproduced except as authorized by written
  permission from the Danish CAA. This restriction extends to all types of media in which the information may be embodied. » An INSPIRE record of 2010 says « no
  conditions apply to access and use »; it is older than the AIP's own text and is not relied on.
- **The form of the data**: the AIP of Denmark is published by Naviair as **PDF only** (ENR 3.2 routes `EK_ENR_3_2_en.pdf`, ENR 2.1, AD 2 procedures, one file per chapter
  and per cycle; the address changes at each AIRAC cycle, the page `aim.naviair.dk/En` lists the current ones). The text of the PDF can be read with the standard library
  (the content streams hold the glyphs and their positions: points, coordinates, limits and distances of ENR 3.2 come out), but the layout is a table spread over pages
  and the file found in the user's message (`mmsbfi0p2t1`) is the **AIRAC 06/24 edition (13 June 2024)**: a reader must first find the current one.
- **The decision of the ergonomics session** (guard kept in the code): the terms of Naviair are noted in the catalogue line **before** any of its points is published. They
  forbid reproduction without written permission: **no reader that stores or republishes Naviair data is written until the Danish CAA answers.**

## 2. Who to write to

- **aim@naviair.dk** (Naviair AIM, Naviair Allé 1, DK-2770 Kastrup: the contact printed in the AIP); the copyright text names the Danish CAA (Trafikstyrelsen) as the party
  whose written permission is needed, so the same message may be copied to it.
