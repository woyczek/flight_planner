- **Proposals of lot 1** (accepted):
  1. The studio and the generator write, per map, `profile_def` (copy of the profile), the resolved `bbox` and the
     `cycle`; the same goes for `vertical_profile.profile_def`. Older missions use the built-in profile of their type.
  2. Fuel writes the climb and descent figures with the flight (`profile: { climbFpm, climbKt, descentFpm, descentKt,
     tocNm, todNm }`), rather than maps computing them a second time.
  3. The frame allows the tile hosts (OpenFreeMap, VersaTiles) and the terrain tiles, and offers a "cache on first
     use" list so that MapLibre (1 MB) is not precached by every phone.
  4. Without tiles (offline, or refused), the map is drawn on a plain background with the country outlines of
     `aero_zones_geo.js`.
  5. Lots: 2 the route map of the planned flight, 3 the vertical profile with the common cursor, 4 the mission maps
     redrawn.
- **22 Oct 2026 — lot 2, the route map of the planned flight** ([SPEC.md](SPEC.md), [INTEGRATION.md](INTEGRATION.md)).
  - **Copies in `lib/`**: the frame's service worker keeps only `frame/` and `modules/`, so `aero_symbols.js` (with a
    header line naming its origin) and MapLibre are copied there. The country outlines are generated by
    `tools/build_maps_outlines.py` (350 KB).
  - **Base map**: OpenFreeMap `positron`, a light style under the ICAO symbology. MapLibre is loaded with a script
    element: it is a UMD bundle, not an ES module.
  - **Display profile**: a built-in "IFR route" profile in the module, derived from the studio's `ifr-simple` and
    `ifrRouteProfile`. Low or high airways and airspaces follow the flight level; aerodromes, navaids and MORA are on.
  - **Overview declutter**: below 120 px per degree, a whole route on a phone was unreadable (every aerodrome and
    VOR rose of France). Only what is within 15 NM of the route is drawn; closer views show everything.
  - **Route on top**: the route's labels are reserved first in the placer, and the route is drawn last. In the first
    version the symbols hid the point labels.
  - **Data area**:
    - overview: the route's area; closer: the view plus 15 %;
    - airspaces and airways: the route's corridor only. In the first version a portrait view of a west-east route
      opened eleven zones.
  - **Plain background**: static, fitted to the route; no pan or zoom without tiles in this lot.
  - **Test page**: `modules/maps/test/index.html` mounts the module outside the frame, which does not list it yet. It
    uses the real data layer (local transport, references from the launcher) and seeds a flight
    (`?flight=ifr|low|none`, `?tiles=0`).
