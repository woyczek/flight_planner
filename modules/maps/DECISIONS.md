- **Proposals of lot 1** (accepted):
  1. The studio and the generator write, per map, `profile_def` (copy of the profile), the resolved `bbox` and the
     `cycle`; the same goes for `vertical_profile.profile_def`. Older missions use the built-in profile of their type.
  2. Fuel writes the climb and descent figures with the flight (`profile: { climbFpm, climbKt, descentFpm, descentKt,
     tocNm, todNm }`), rather than maps computing them a second time.
  3. The frame allows the tile hosts (OpenFreeMap, VersaTiles) and the terrain tiles, and offers a "cache on first
     use" list so that MapLibre (1 MB) is not precached by every phone.
  4. Without tiles (offline, or refused), the map is drawn on a plain background with the country outlines of
     `aero_zones_geo.js`.
  5. Lots: 2 the route map of the planned flight, 3 the vertical profile with the common cursor, 4 the mission maps
     redrawn.
- **22 Oct 2026 — lot 2, the route map of the planned flight** ([SPEC.md](SPEC.md), [INTEGRATION.md](INTEGRATION.md)).
  - **Copies in `lib/`**: the frame's service worker keeps only `frame/` and `modules/`, so `aero_symbols.js` (with a
    header line naming its origin) and MapLibre are copied there. The country outlines are generated by
    `tools/build_maps_outlines.py` (350 KB).
  - **Base map**: OpenFreeMap `positron`, a light style under the ICAO symbology. MapLibre is loaded with a script
    element: it is a UMD bundle, not an ES module.
  - **Display profile**: a built-in "IFR route" profile in the module, derived from the studio's `ifr-simple` and
    `ifrRouteProfile`. Low or high airways and airspaces follow the flight level; aerodromes, navaids and MORA are on.
  - **Overview declutter**: below 120 px per degree, a whole route on a phone was unreadable (every aerodrome and
    VOR rose of France). Only what is within 15 NM of the route is drawn; closer views show everything.
  - **Route on top**: the route's labels are reserved first in the placer, and the route is drawn last. In the first
    version the symbols hid the point labels.
  - **Data area**:
    - overview: the route's area; closer: the view plus 15 %;
    - airspaces and airways: the route's corridor only. In the first version a portrait view of a west-east route
      opened eleven zones.
  - **Plain background**: static, fitted to the route; no pan or zoom without tiles in this lot.
  - **Test page**: `modules/maps/test/index.html` mounts the module outside the frame, which does not list it yet. It
    uses the real data layer (local transport, references from the launcher) and seeds a flight
    (`?flight=ifr|low|none`, `?tiles=0`).

- **22 Oct 2026 — lot 3, the vertical profile** ([SPEC.md](SPEC.md) § 4 bis).
  - **The studio's profile code** is copied unchanged (`lib/vertical_profile.js`); `profile.js` adds fuel's trajectory,
    the MORA fallback, the cursor geometry and what lies under the cursor.
  - **Fuel's trajectory first**: `tocNm` and `todNm` come from fuel's quote. Drawing them as they are keeps the map,
    the profile and fuel's result in agreement. `SiaProfile`'s own plan is the fallback.
  - **Only the airspaces the trajectory enters**: at FL270 from Nantes to Marseille, every TMA and CTA under the route
    made the 351 px profile unreadable. The strip then lists, as "the airspace under the finger", the ones at the
    planned altitude, which is what a pilot asks.
  - **Terrain at zoom 9** (about 300 m, half the tiles of the studio's zoom 10); the highest point within 1 km either
    side is kept, as in the studio. Offline: the MORA of the cells crossed, said in the status line.
  - **Cursor**: on the profile, pointer events (`touch-action: pan-y`, so a vertical swipe still scrolls the page); on
    the map, a tap near the route, never a drag (dragging pans the map).
  - **Corridor leg by leg** for airspaces and airways (2 NM for the profile, 12 NM for the map).
  - **Menu entry** added in `frame/app.js` after fuel, as the ergonomics session asked.
  - **Layout**: map 52 % of the screen height (at least 260 px), profile 30 % (200 to 340 px), the strip under it.

- **22 Oct 2026 — lot 4, the mission maps** ([SPEC.md](SPEC.md) § 4 ter).
  - **Specification for V2** (sent to the ergonomics session, made by BUGS SOG on develop ea077ae36, reported on v3
    f799f8429):
    - `profile_def`, `resolved_bbox`, `cycle` in each `maps[]` entry;
    - `profile_def`, `cycle`, `route_points` in `vertical_profile`;
    - the registry ignores them only in those two places, and carries them over map by map on a new version.
    - `resolved_bbox` is a key of its own: `bbox` stays an input that the studio reads as a constraint.
  - **Missions read as "reference"**: listed from the tree, only the chosen YAML read.
  - **Built-in profiles generated, not copied by hand**: `tools/build_maps_profiles.py` extracts `defaultProfiles`
    from the studio and derives `ifr-low` and `ifr-high` as `ifrRouteProfile` does. The `vprof` profile keeps every
    airspace type.
  - **No overview declutter on mission maps**: their profile was chosen for that map; what it asks for is drawn.
  - **Base styles**: the studio's own vector style, OSM and OpenTopoMap need hosts outside `meta.net`, so they become
    `positron`; the OpenFreeMap and VersaTiles styles are used as named.
  - **Route levels kept**: `route_points.alt_ft`, and the levels of the parsed route text, drive the mission's vertical
    profile (FL240 to PT1, then FL260 from PT3 in the test mission).
  - **Layout fix**: a long map description made its chooser wider than the screen; the choosers now shrink (ellipsis).
  - **Seen in the data, not in the code**: a town labelled "Béziers" near Châtellerault comes from the published towns
    (GeoNames).

- **23 Oct 2026 — designs, day and night** (design lot 2, `frame/design/INTEGRATION.md`).
  - **A colour-mapping context rather than edited copies**:
    - the studio's symbols and profile write white, ink and greys by value, and the copies stay unchanged;

    - the module's own code uses the tokens directly (`paper()`, `S.theme`).

    - the OpenFreeMap `dark` base, since light tiles would stay white;
    - dark cartographic colours lightened by 45 %. Before that, blue aerodrome codes and purple route labels on dark

  - **Checked through `frame/index.html`** at 375 px, flight map and mission map, in standard, cabinet and field, day
    and night (12 runs, design set in `device/design.json`). The profile background equals `--sog-chart-bg` in each

- **23 Oct 2026 — lot 5: airway points, presets, density, tooltips, live view.**
  - **Reference**: Navigraph high-level IFR charts given by the user (boxed airway names along the segment, minimum
    level, segment distance, tracks, fixes as triangles, MORA "3⁵").
  - **Airway points**: the ends of the segments drawn. A navaid at the same place (within 0.05°) is drawn with its own
    symbol, even when the profile hides navaids; the other points are triangles. Their names pass before the track
    labels, which are placed last.
  - **Presets rather than a free profile editor**: four choices cover the captures (low, high, VFR) and the automatic
    one. Auto follows the level of the current flight (FL195, as the V2 `ifrRouteProfile`). The VFR preset takes the
    studio's `vfr-detail` profile on the topographic `ofm-liberty` base. The choice is per device, in the frame's
    cache (`cache/maps/ui.json`), never synchronised.
  - **Density by zoom** replaces part of the overview rule: three tiers by pixels per degree (80, 300). The thresholds
    were set from 375 px screenshots: the fixes appear from the mid tier, otherwise a phone showing France never sees
    them.
  - **Tooltips on the label's place**:
    - the airspace tooltip uses AIP notation, the same in the line under the profile (before: "FL 195 – FL 999");
    - airway tooltip asked by the user ("no rollover on airway names on the map"): `draw()` returns the boxes it
      placed, so the hit test uses exactly what is drawn, and needs no second layout;
    - touch: Chrome sends compatibility mouse events after a tap; they reopened the map's tooltip 450 ms after the tap
      that closed it, and the map's `mouseout` hid the profile's. Both are ignored now, and `mouseout` hides only the
      map's tooltip.
  - **`alt_ft` is a held level, not an altitude to reach**: fuel lot C defines it as the level from the point on
    (OFP "/F280"). It is passed to the profile as `lvl`, with `level.ft` on the first point; with `alt`, the profile
    would reach the level at the point and the steps would start one segment early. Missions keep the studio's
    meaning (altitude at the point).
  - **Bench**:
    - Chrome kept a 150 % zoom for host 127.0.0.1, so a "1280 px" emulation gave 853 CSS px (and "375 px" gave 250).
      The benches now run on `localhost`, which has no saved zoom;

      the map 210 px wide);
