# maps — specification (regeneration prompt)

This document describes the V3 map module completely enough for a developer or a language model to rebuild it without
seeing its code. Reference implementation: `modules/maps/module.js` (the screen), `modules/maps/render.js` (the
rendering core cut out of the V2 studio), `modules/maps/profile.js` (the vertical profile), `modules/maps/mission.js`
(a mission's maps), `modules/maps/lib/` (copies: ICAO symbols, the studio's profile code, MapLibre; generated: country
outlines, built-in display profiles).
Reading of the V2 studio: [READING.md](READING.md). How it is mounted and what it needs: [INTEGRATION.md](INTEGRATION.md).
Choices and measurements: [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md).

## 1. Purpose

The Pilot application's map, read only, fast on a phone:
- the route map of the planned flight (lot 2) and its vertical profile with a common cursor (lot 3);
- the mission maps redrawn from what the mission stores (lot 4).

## 2. Inputs

- **The planned flight**: `pilot/<trigram>/current_flight.json` (written by fuel, schema in
  `modules/fuel/INTEGRATION.md`). Used: `route.points` [{ id, lat, lon, alt_ft? }], `route.dep`, `route.arr`,
  `route.altn`, `level.ft`, `aircraft.type`, `profile` { climbFpm, climbKt, descentFpm, descentKt, tocNm, todNm }.
  Nothing is written there.
  - `alt_ft` (fuel § 3): the level held from that point on, never on the first or the last point. The flight leaves
    at `level.ft`, and a point without `alt_ft` keeps the previous level. It becomes the point's `lvl` for the profile
    (like "/F240" in an OFP route): steps, TOC and TOD per segment; fuel's single-level `tocNm` / `todNm` are then not
    used. The map writes the level next to the point's name ("LMG · FL280").
- **The display setting**: `cache/maps/ui.json` { preset } (frame cache, per device).
- **Terrain**: Terrarium elevation tiles (AWS, zoom 9, about 300 m), read by the profile code itself (`meta.net`).
- **Aeronautical data**: `ctx.aerodb`, by area only.
- **Base map**: OpenFreeMap tiles (style `positron`) drawn by MapLibre; MapLibre calls the tile hosts itself (the
  hosts are listed in `meta.net`), never through `ctx.net`.
- **Country outlines** (`lib/outlines.js`, generated by `tools/build_maps_outlines.py` from
  `shared/carto/aero_zones_geo.js`): the plain background when tiles are unavailable.

## 3. Rendering core (`render.js`, `window.MapsRender`)

Everything is a parameter; nothing is read from a page or a data global. The projection is given:
`toC(lat, lon) → { x, y }` (MapLibre's `map.project`, or the Mercator projection of the plain background).

- `routeProfile(levelFt)` → the built-in "IFR route" display profile. It has the studio's profile shape (`zones`,
  `zoneList`, `zoneMode`, `corridorNm`, `elements`).
  - Airspaces: R, D, P, TRA, CTR, TMA, CTA, within 10 NM of the route.
  - Below FL195: low airways, and airspaces below 19,500 ft. From FL195: high airways, and airspaces above.
  - Aerodromes (public and state), VOR / TACAN / DME / NDB, MORA, graticule.
- `selectAirspaces(list, profile, route)` → the airspaces to draw:
  - kept by family, floor and ceiling (`SiaAero.limitFt`), and the white or black list;
  - then the corridor: a route sample inside the airspace, or an edge within `corridorNm`.
- `bboxOf(points, marginNm)`, `distances(points)` (great circle, NM), `marks(points, stepNm)` (every 10 NM, or every
  50 NM beyond 150 NM), `mercator(bbox, W, H, pad)` → `{ toC, shown }`.
- `drawBackground(ctx, toC, W, H, rings)`: sea, land, borders.
- `draw(ctx, { toC, W, H, scale, bbox, profile, data, route, marks })`. Drawing order:
  1. the route's labels are reserved first in the shared label placer (`SiaAero.placer`);
  2. airspace shapes (ICAO symbology, `SiaAero.drawAirspaces`);
  3. airways;
  4. aerodromes and navaids (`SiaAero.drawPoints`);
  5. airspace labels;
  6. graticule and MORA (the highest MORA of 2, 4… degree groups when a degree is narrower than 70 px);
  7. the route on top: line, points, NM marks and their labels.
- **Overview rule** (under 120 px per degree): only aerodromes and VOR / TACAN within 15 NM of the route, airways
  whose middle is within 30 NM, no obstacles. Closer views show everything the profile allows.
- **Density by zoom** (`tierOf(px per degree)`: far under 80, mid under 300, near beyond; `densify(data, tier,
  profile)`; mission maps are always drawn in full; with `vfrDensity`, VRP at every tier):
  - far: large aerodromes only, no navaids, airways with their boxed names;
  - mid: plus the airway points and the minimum level under each name ("F195");
  - near: everything, plus each segment's distance (NM) above the name, the true tracks near both ends ("097°"), VRP
    and obstacles.
- **Airways** (`drawAirways`), as en-route charts: low airways solid blue, high airways dashed purple; the name in a
  box laid along the segment (always readable left to right). The segment ends are drawn after them
  (`drawFixes`: a triangle and the id; a navaid at the same place is drawn with its own symbol instead). The boxes
  drawn are returned by `draw()` as `{ airways: [{ x, y, r, g }] }` for the tooltips.
- **AIP notation** (`sia(airspace)` → [main line, activation · information]; `siaLimit`): "TMA 8 AQUITAINE 2000|FL145
  D": SFC, FL### (FL999 → UNL), ASFC, plain feet AMSL. Airways: `awLimit(ft)` (FL from 5,000 ft, UNL from 66,000 ft).
  `airspacesAt(list, lat, lon)` and `airwaysAt(hits, x, y)` give what is under a point.

## 4. Screen (`module.js`)

- **Header**: title, "Edit the route" (opens `#fuel`), "Recentre", then the summary "dep → arr · alternate · level ·
  distance · type".
- **The map**:
  - 60 % of the screen height (at least 280 px), with the aeronautical layer on a canvas over MapLibre;
  - pan and pinch, no rotation, no pitch, Mercator, north up;
  - redrawn on every move (one frame at a time);
  - the alternate: a grey dashed line from the arrival, a grey dot and its code.
- **Data area**:
  - in overview (view at least 90 % as wide as the route's area), the route's area;
  - closer, the view widened by 15 %; read again only when the view leaves it;
  - airspaces and airways are read for the route's corridor only (`corridorNm` + 2 NM), leg by leg: one rectangle
    around a diagonal route would touch neighbouring zones (Spain for Nantes → Marseille).
- **Base map**: MapLibre is loaded at the first map (script and style sheet from `lib/`).
  - With no network, no WebGL, an error, or no style after 10 s, the map uses the plain background. It is fitted to
    the route and static.
  - The status line says "Base map unavailable: plain background".
- **Empty states**, written only when they change:
  - no flight: the map is hidden, with a message and "Open Fuel";
  - no published database: the route alone;
  - offline with no data for the area: a message and "Offline data" (`#settings`).
- **Updates**:
  - the flight file is watched (`ctx.data.watch`): a new `updated_at` refits the map and reads again;
  - `aerodb.on('change')`: the data are read again;
  - `ResizeObserver`: resize and redraw.
- **Map modes** (the user's validated mock-up, adapted to the `--sog-*` tokens; hidden for mission maps): a trigger
  at the top left (current mode, chevron) opens a grid of six choices with icon and label, 72 px high, six per row,
  three when the map box is 460 px wide or less (container query); the Layers button stays under the trigger.
  - Auto (wand, the flight's level: High IFR from FL195), Low IFR ("IFR" under a ceiling, `routeProfile(5000)`), High
    IFR ("IFR" over a floor, `routeProfile(30000)`), VFR (map icon, the built-in `vfr-detail` profile on the
    `ofm-liberty` base), Topo (contour lines drawn in CSS: `ofm-liberty` with a hillshade of the Terrarium tiles,
    aerodromes, towns and close-up obstacles only), CAM / OAT (red plane: the flight level's IFR plus refuelling and
    NAT tracks, R / D / P, TSA /
    TRA / CBA and the low-flying network at all levels, state aerodromes).
  - Icons from `frame/lib/icons.js` (`wand-sparkles` added, `map`, `plane`, `chevron-down`); colours: accent for the
    choice, `--sog-err` for CAM; corners from `--sog-radius` (square in cabinet and field).
  - Kept in `cache/maps/ui.json` (`preset`: auto, low, high, vfr, topo, cam); each mode has its own layers.
- **Tooltips**, a `.mp-tip` card in the map box or the profile box:
  - touch (or click): the airway whose box is under the finger (route, point → point, distance, tracks both ways,
    floor|ceiling, low or high), else the airspaces shown under the point in AIP notation (activation and information
    below), else "No airspace shown here"; it stays until the next tap or a move of the map (a tap near the route
    places the cursor instead);
  - mouse (class `mp-hover`): only over an airway box or an airspace label (`draw()` returns both frames), at once,
    following the pointer 12 px away (never under it, kept inside the box); once the pointer leaves the label or the
    map, it stays 5 s at its last place; a new hover replaces it, a click closes it; lighter: `--sog-chart-bg` at
    65 %, `--sog-fg`, thin border, 11.5 px, two lines at most;
  - the compatibility mouse events that follow a touch are ignored (`sourceCapabilities.firesTouchEvents`, or within
    1 s of a touch);
  - profile: the airspaces crossed at the cursor; touch as above, mouse as above with the first two and "+n more";
    the line under the profile uses the same notation;
  - an airspace without limits in the base (FIR, UTA) shows no "SFC|SFC".
- **Layers panel** (button with a layers icon under the presets; hidden for mission maps): airspace families (TMA /
  CTR: TMA, CTR, CTA, TMZ, RMZ; D / R / P: R, D, P, TRA, TSA, CBA, PJE, TRVL, TRPVL, ZIT; FIR: FIR, UIR, UTA, LTA, OCA,
  FRA; others: every other type present), lower and upper airways, beacons, airway points (`fixes`), VRP, obstacles,
  MORA, towns, graticule, alternate, and "Around the route only".
  - Defaults come from the preset's profile (a family is on when the profile names one of its types); the choices
    are kept per preset and per device in `cache/maps/ui.json` `{ preset, layers: { preset: { key: bool } } }`, only
    where they differ from the defaults; "Reset all" removes them.
  - The effective profile (`effectiveProfile()`) is the preset's with the layers applied: elements, `zones` (whole
    families), `otherZones`, `corridorNm`.
- **Around the route only**: on by default when the preset's profile has a corridor (IFR), off in VFR. Off, the data
  area follows the view (widened by 15 %) and airspaces and airways are read and drawn over all of it. In a close view
  (300 px per degree or more), everything in the view is read and drawn whatever the layer says.
- **VFR preset**: the studio's `vfr-detail` profile on the `ofm-liberty` base, plus beacons and `vfrDensity` (VRP at
  every zoom). Its families come from the layers: TMA / CTR and D / R / P whole (the studio's profile only names D, P,
  CTR and PJE, so a close view outside a CTR was bare).
- **Live view**: every write of the flight file redraws the map and the profile (debounced 300 ms,
  `performance.mark('maps:live')`); the view stays when the new route still fits in it; no zone is read again unless
  the data area changes.
- **Refuelling and NAT tracks** (`aarTracks`, `natTracks` elements; layers "Air refuelling tracks (AAR)" and "NAT
  tracks", on by default in CAM only; mission maps whose profile asks for them): read after the zones of the view
  (`aerodb.aarTracks()`, `aerodb.natTracks()`, which opens the `nat` zone); racetrack dashed in dark green with a light
  tint, outbound leg IP → CP as an arrow, the track's name beside it; heavy tracks as their two arrowed legs; NAT
  tracks dashed in slate through their points, "NAT A FL350–400" at the first point.
- **Profile marks**: the route's points, and the airway points and navaids within 3 NM of the route (at least 30 px
  apart), as small triangles with their id.
- **TOC and TOD on the map**: their label goes north-east of the diamond, else south-east, north-west, south-west,
  wherever it does not cover a route label (`draw()` returns their frames).
- **"Edit the route"** opens `#fuel?from=maps&focus=route` (fuel's Route step, text field focused).
- **Editing the route on the map** (lot 6a; hidden for mission maps): an "Edit" toggle at the top right of the map
  box, with "Undo" beside it in the mode; the line under the profile gives the gestures.
  - A press within 22 px of a waypoint (not departure nor arrival) drags it: the press is caught before MapLibre
    (`pointerdown` in the capture phase, `mousedown` / `touchstart` / `touchmove` stopped while dragging), so the map
    does not pan; anywhere else the map pans as usual.
  - While dragging: rubber band from the previous to the next point, the anchor under the finger (navaid, aerodrome,
    airway point, RNAV point, VRP of the data read, within 20 px) ringed with its id. Released on it: the point takes
    its id and position, and keeps its `alt_ft`. Released elsewhere: a free point at the finger's position, named by its ICAO
    coordinates (`icaoCoord`, "4530N00030E"), for VFR and helicopters; an anchor within 20 px keeps the priority.
  - A tap on an anchor off the route inserts it where it lengthens the route least; a tap on a route point opens its
    card (id, departure / arrival or the level held from it, "Remove" for a waypoint); the click that follows a drag
    is ignored.
  - Levels (lot 6b): in the mode, a vertical drag on the profile (more than 6 px) sets the level held on the segment
    under the finger, in steps of 1,000 ft, shown as a dashed line with its level while dragging; a tap without
    dragging places the cursor as usual. The level applies from the point that opens the segment: on the first
    segment it is `level.ft`, elsewhere the point's `alt_ft`, removed when it equals the previous segment's level. In
    the mode the profile has `touch-action: none` (the page does not scroll under the finger). A point's card shows
    the level held from it with "−" and "+" (FL10).
  - "Undo" restores the points and the flight level before the last gesture (20 kept, in memory).
  - Each gesture is shown at once (points, header, profile); 400 ms after the last one, the record is written:
    `route.points` (rounded to 1e-6°), `route.text` from fuel's `textFromPoints(points, previousText,
    previousPoints)` (`frame/lib/route_text.js`: the airway between two neighbouring points left unchanged is kept,
    the one of a changed segment drops; ICAO coordinates for a point without a usable name), `level.ft` when the first
    segment's level changed, `distanceNm`, `trackDeg`, `from: 'points'`, `manual: false`, `by: 'maps'`, `updated_at`,
    merged into the latest version read (no other field lost).
  - The live view takes a version of the record only if it is newer than the one shown: the write of an earlier
    gesture, coming back after newer gestures, would otherwise undo them.
  - While the record is not `by: 'fuel'`, the profile ignores fuel's `profile` (TOC, TOD) and computes its own;
    fuel redoes the quote and writes it back (`modules/fuel/INTEGRATION.md` § 3).
- **Texts**: four languages, through `ctx.t`. Data labels (codes, names, levels) are not translated.

## 4 bis. Vertical profile (`profile.js`, `window.MapsProfile`)

- **Cut**: `SiaProfile.build` (copy of the studio's code). 100 to 800 samples along the route; terrain is the highest
  point on the line and 1 km either side; airspaces crossed are the sample intervals inside their rings.
- **Airspaces**: those of the route profile's types along the route (2 NM boxes, leg by leg), without the floor and
  ceiling filter. Only those that the planned trajectory enters are kept. Limits relative to the ground (ASFC) are
  raised by the terrain. Without a trajectory (no level), all are kept.
- **Trajectory**:
  - with fuel's `profile` and a level: linear climb from the departure's terrain to the level over `tocNm`, cruise,
    linear descent to the arrival's terrain over `todNm` (fuel's own climb and descent);
  - otherwise `SiaProfile`'s own plan, with fuel's rates if given, else 1,000 ft/min at 140 kt up and 800 ft/min at
    160 kt down.
  TOC and TOD are the ends of the cruise.
- **Terrain unavailable** (offline, or the first tile refused): the cut has no terrain. The MORA of each 1° cell
  crossed is drawn as a dashed brown step line (aerodb `mora`), and the status line says so.
- **Drawing**: `SiaProfile.draw` (axes in ft / FL, airspace bands and labels, terrain, points with their distances,
  trajectory with TOC / TOD and the cruise level), scale 0.78 under 500 px. Then:
  - the NM marks of the map, as small ticks at the bottom of the frame;
  - the cursor, on its own canvas: a vertical line and a dot at the planned altitude.
- **Common cursor**:
  - a finger or the mouse on the profile sets the distance;
  - a tap on the map within 32 px of the route sets it to the nearest sample;
  - the map shows it as a blue ring, with TOC and TOD as purple diamonds.
- **The strip under the profile**: distance (NM) · planned altitude · terrain or MORA · the airspaces under the cursor
  (type, name, limits), or "outside the airspaces shown". Without a cursor, a one-line hint.
- **API**: `build(points, { levelFt, profile, airspaces, elevation, mora })`, `draw(ctx, cut, { W, H, scale })` →
  geometry, `xOf(geo, s)`, `sOf(geo, x)`, `at(cut, s)`, `posAt(cut, s)`, `nearest(cut, toC, x, y, maxPx)`; distances in
  metres, altitudes in feet.

## 4 ter. Mission maps (`mission.js`, `window.MapsMission`)

- **Address**: `#maps?mission=<ID>&map=<file>`. Without `map`, the mission's first map; without `mission`, the planned
  flight.
- **Choosers**: in the header, "Planned flight" then every mission of the repository; for a mission with two maps or
  more, a second chooser with its maps (description, else file name). Choosing changes the address; the frame mounts
  the module again.
- **Missions**: `missions/<l>_<FAMILY>/<ID>/mission<ID>.yaml`.
  - The list comes from the data layer's tree (`list('missions/')`); nothing is read to list them.
  - Only the chosen mission's YAML is read (policy "reference"), and parsed with `frame/lib/js-yaml.min.js`.
  - A change to that file (`watch`) reads it again.
- **Parsing** (the studio's rules, `parseMissionYaml`):
  - points: origin, destination, target, alternate, turning points, rendezvous;
  - zones: circle or polygon; kind search, exclusion or mission;
  - `maps[]`: file, type, description, centre (`centre_ref` or `center`), `bbox` / `range_km` / `width_km` /
    `height_km`, `view`, `profile`, `trace`; maps with profile `off` are left out;
  - `vertical_profile`: route, cruise, rates.
- **Fields written by the V2 studio** (BUGS SOG, develop ea077ae36, v3 f799f8429):
  - `profile_def`: the full display profile, used as is;
  - `resolved_bbox`: the extent drawn;
  - `cycle`: the AIRAC cycle of the map. A cycle different from aerodb's is said in the status line.
  - In `vertical_profile`: `profile_def`, `cycle`, `route_points` [{ name, lat, lon, alt_ft }].
- **Without those fields** (older missions):
  - **Profile**: the built-in profile of the same id, else the built-in profile of the type. Detail, target and rdv
    give `vfr-detail`; approach gives `vfr-simple`; anything else gives `ifr-simple`. The status line says so.
  - **Extent**: `view`, else `bbox`, else the automatic frame by type:
    - general: points and zones, 12 % margin;
    - zone: the zone, 20 % margin;
    - detail: 8 × 5 km; target: 100 × 62 km; others: 220 × 140 km;
    - widened to the studio's 1.6 ratio.
  - **Route**: `vertical_profile.route` (or origin, turning points or target, destination), parsed by
    `SiaProfile.parseRoute`. Names resolve against the mission's points and zones, then aerodb's `coords`. The levels
    of the text (FL240 then FL260) are kept.
- **Drawing**: the map's profile decides everything: base style (OpenFreeMap and VersaTiles styles; the studio's own
  style, OSM and OpenTopoMap become `positron`), airspaces, airways, aerodromes, navaids, obstacles, VRP and RNAV
  points, cities, MORA, graticule. Then:
  - the mission's zones (hatched, dashed outline, name);
  - the mission's points (dot and name, labels reserved first);
  - the route when `trace` is `route` or `both`, or by default when the mission draws its route on maps.
  No overview declutter: a mission map shows what its profile asks for.
- **Vertical profile**: the mission's route with its levels and rates. Airspaces come from the `vprof` profile (copy,
  else built-in: every type). The common cursor works as for the flight.
- **Not drawn** (later, if asked): probable route and exercise tracks, operation maps, AAR tracks, NAT, built-up
  areas, summits, relief and contour layers.

## 4 quater. Designs, day and night (frame/design)

- **Page**: frame primitives only (`f-tools`, `f-btn`, `f-muted`); the module's own style only places things.
  Backgrounds use `--sog-bg` (map box) and `--sog-chart-bg` (profile); the credit line uses `--sog-chart-bg` and
  `--sog-fg`.
- **Canvas**: `MapsRender.tokens()` reads `--sog-chart-bg`, `--sog-bg`, `--sog-fg`, `--sog-muted`,
  `--sog-chart-grid` and `--sog-accent` with `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement)`. Night is a chart background
  darker than 45 % luminance.
  - `MapsRender.themed(ctx, tokens)` wraps the 2D context. Paper colours written by value, in this module and in the
    studio's copies, are replaced as they are set:
    - white (backgrounds, halos, label boxes, any alpha) → `--sog-chart-bg`;
    - ink → `--sog-fg`; greys → `--sog-muted`; rules → `--sog-chart-grid`;
    - at night, a dark cartographic colour is lightened by 45 % toward white, alpha kept.
    Other cartographic colours pass unchanged.
  - The cursor (ring on the map, line and dot on the profile) is `--sog-accent`.
  - The plain background is sea `--sog-bg`, land `--sog-chart-bg`, borders `--sog-chart-grid`.
- **Base map at night**: OpenFreeMap `dark` (VersaTiles profiles: `eclipse`).
- **Changes**: a change of `data-design` or `data-tone` on `<html>`, or of `prefers-color-scheme`, reads the tokens
  again. The module switches the base style when night changes, then redraws the map and the profile.

## 5. Invariants

1. Read only: the module writes nothing (`meta.writes` is empty).
2. A module file is never read from outside `modules/maps/` or the frame (the service worker keeps only those); V2
   files are copied into `lib/`, unchanged, with their origin in a header line.
3. No interface text in `render.js`.
4. The plain background always works offline (core, symbols and outlines are in `meta.files`).
