# Missions

The airline's missions, in the Company application: those of the shared repository (written with the CPCO): choose one, or draw one at random, then prepare it in Fuel.

## How to use

<a id="missions-assigned"></a>**My assigned missions.** When the airline's centre assigns you a mission, it appears at the head of Missions, under "My assigned missions": the take-off (aerodrome, time Z), the aircraft, the callsign, and the crew's rendezvous and channels when the assignment carries them ("Open" for a link, "Copy" for a server or a frequency); "Prepare this flight" opens it in Fuel. The group exists only when an assignment carries your trigram; a cancelled assignment, or one whose take-off is more than 12 h past, is no longer there. Assignments are readable by every pilot of the airline: keep session passwords for the channel itself.

<a id="missions-list"></a>**The list.** The missions of the newest series first, grouped by family. Only the missions whose file is on this device are offered, in the list as in the draw; when there is none, the screen says "No readable mission on this device: synchronise". Each row gives the id, the title, the route, the aircraft type and the estimated block; "flown on …" marks a mission whose report you wrote. The search filters on the id, the title, an aerodrome (ICAO code), an aircraft type, a callsign (SAMU66) or the activity (MEDEVAC, HEMS, SAMU…), and also in the content of the missions already on the device: summary, description, objectives, risks, places, notes, callsigns and types, ignoring case, accents and hyphens ("clermont-ferrand" = "Clermont Ferrand"). A place that only a drawer cites is not searched, even once the drawer is open: the search never gives a closed drawer away. When the word is not in the title, the row says where it was found ("in: risks"); a mission whose file is not on the device yet is searched on its registry line, and the list says so ("n missions not read"). Nothing is written or downloaded to search. The registry search is the same; the drop-down list keeps one family, given by its letter (the one of the identifiers), its short name and its name ("T · AIRLIFT · Airlift / logistics"), in letter order; a chosen family with several subtypes opens a second one, "Subtype", beside it. Seven rows are shown, "N more" unfolds the rest. The organisation missions of an exercise (those ending in Z) are not flights and are not listed. Only the missions of the airline chosen in Settings › Airline are listed; a mission that names no airline is personal: it is under « Personnel ». The list « My airline · Personal · All airlines », above the search, also shows the personal missions or those of other airlines, each with the name of its airline; they can be prepared the same way. When the airline has categories (cargo, passengers…), a second drop-down keeps one category, and the open mission says its own.

<a id="missions-open"></a>**Choose, or draw at random.** A tap on a row opens the mission sheet, its Brief: its category, its line of choice (duration · aircraft · theatre, as in the registry), its summary in full under the title "Summary" (beyond 600 signs, "Read more" opens the detail), its risks (the first three, "hazard → mitigation", the others folded, or "No risk declared"), the teams engaged and its zones. "Detail" shows the rest in foldable sections: description (the detailed description only, without the summary), objectives, zones to activate, rendezvous, route provided, maps provided, payload and aircraft; "See on the Map" opens the Map on a zone or on a map of the mission (drawn from its coordinates). On a phone, the browser's back returns from the detail to the sheet, then to the list; on a large screen, the sheet and the detail are side by side. "Draw at random" picks one among the missions of the filtered list you have not flown yet; "Draw again" picks another. Nothing starts by itself: "Prepare this flight" opens Fuel, in the Pilot application, with the departure, the arrival, the points of the mission (as coordinates), the level and the aircraft type; the runway, the procedures, the fuel and the weather are then prepared as for any flight.

<a id="missions-drawers"></a>**The rest of the mission.** A mission may have a sequel that nothing announces: it is given to you in flight. On the sheet of YOUR mission — it is assigned to you, or it is the mission of your flight — it then appears under “Rest of the mission”: its title, how and when (“opened on entering the zone, at 20:10Z”), its message and its briefing; a notification says so. Nothing appears before. “Ask for what comes next”, next to “Prepare this flight”, is touched when you have reached or found what the mission asks for: the centre gives you what comes next if there is anything, otherwise it answers “Nothing new for now”; the command then rests for a minute. During the mission's flight, with a live position (this computer's simulator, the GPS, your online session), your device follows the flight without saying anything; a landing asked for by the mission also counts when your device knows you are on the ground (simulator, IVAO), and so does an overflight, or a hover held within a band of heights above the ground, when it knows it (simulator only), and a hot refuelling — down at the station, on the heading asked, for the time asked, then gone again (simulator only); a sequence of steps opens when all are done, in order; without a live position, what comes next arrives at its time. A precision landing on a point is judged in tiers — excellent, perfect, good, partial — which the line of the sequel states with the distance to the target point; for an arrival at an airfield and for a required stopover, the margin is fixed, no longer the radius written in the mission file. What is given to a crew mate on their finding is given to you too — within a few seconds while your sheet is on screen, and the line says “received here at …” when it came later. At the Full level, “Reseal” closes again a part you opened, for you only. As long as a part is not given, the sheet and its Detail neither list nor name what it hides; a mission you are not playing shows only its visible part. The device keeps to itself what was given to you (nothing is sent): the Map uses it. When a part has opened on a precision landing, a block “Debrief” appears under the sequel: one line per landing judged, yours and your crew mates', with the tier and the distance to the target point, computed from your own mission file.

<a id="missions-assign"></a>**Assigning a mission.** At the Everyday level, “Assign…” in the sheet of a mission opens “Assign this mission”: the pilot (a trigram of the airline, or three letters), the callsign, the take-off in UTC and the aircraft — a type of the mission and, if you wish, a registration; the departure aerodrome is the mission's. If the pilot already has a mission less than two hours away, the screen says so and you can assign anyway. The assignments of the mission are listed at the top; “Remove” cancels one. The pilot finds the mission in “My assigned missions”. No one checks rights or availability: they remain with your airline's organisation.

<a id="missions-add"></a>**Add missions.** « Add missions », next to « Draw at random », reads one or more YAML mission files (a file may hold several, separated by « --- » or in a `missions:` list), or a pasted YAML — or an assistant’s whole answer: its code blocks (« ```yaml … ``` », else « ``` … ``` ») are read, each as a source, and the text around them is ignored (the screen says so); an indentation with tabs is refused (YAML is indented with spaces) and an error is given with the line of the pasted text. Before writing, the screen shows what it read (id, title, family, series, airline) and what it refuses, with the reason (id outside the rule, exercise organisation, duplicate, YAML unreadable at a given line). « Add (n) » writes each mission at its place, `missions/<letter>_<FAMILY>/<ID>/mission<ID>.yaml`; a mission that already exists is replaced only when « replace? » is ticked. A mission without an airline is personal. They leave at the next synchronisation. The missions added are entered in the registry at once: they open in the workshop without « Update the registry ».

<a id="missions-airline"></a>**Change airline.** On a mission’s sheet, « Airline: … · Change » offers the airlines and « Personal »; the sentence « … moves from … to … » is said before « Write ». In the list, « Assign to an airline… » takes the missions shown (scope, family, search), each ticked: the screen says how many change and which are unreadable (left as they are) before writing. When adding from YAML, « Add these missions to » chooses their airline. Only the `airline` field of the file changes.

<a id="missions-registry"></a>**The registry.** « Registry », next to « Draw at random » (or the address `#missions?view=registry`), shows one line per mission as the registry `missions/mission_registry.yaml` writes it, with the state of its file: there, missing, unreadable or path refused; and the repository missions the registry does not know. On a computer the registry is a table: the state of the mission (not in the registry, to correct, read, not read — the screen reads 500 files at most and says so in the count), the identifier, the title, the family, the series, the origin and the destination, the duration, then « ⋯ »; each column title sorts (a second tap reverses); on a phone, the same list, one mission per card, and a single « Sort » button that opens the choice of the column and the direction. The default order is the identifier, the most recent on top; the sort chosen is kept on this device, and « Reset the order » goes back to the default. Touching a line opens its sheet, on the right of the table (full screen on a phone, « ← Registry » or the browser's Back returns to the list): the state in words and the file, the route, the family, the series, the number, the operation, the duration, the aircraft and the theatre, then « Brief » (the mission sheet as the pilot sees it, never the content of a closed drawer; « Back to the registry » returns to the same sheet), « Workshop », « Duplicate » and « ⋯ » (« Correct the registry line », « Remove from the registry »). « ⋯ » at the end of a line gives the same gestures without leaving the table. A repository mission the registry does not know (added before the import entered it) has, in its sheet and its « ⋯ », « Enter in the registry »: its line is written from its file alone; opening the registry never writes anything. At the head of the registry, each draft of the workshop kept on this device has its line: « Resume » reopens the workshop as you left it, « Discard » erases it after a confirmation. The line of choice says what the mission file says; a missing duration is computed on the route given, at the cruise speed of the file or at a typical speed of the aircraft's class (helicopter, aeroplane), rounded to five minutes and marked « ≈ »; the theatre is the file's, else the State of the departure aerodrome; what is not known is not shown. The family filter (and its subtype, as in the list) and the search are at the head. « Update the registry » first says what it would do (missions to add, lines without a readable file, duplicate identifiers, former identifiers), then « Write the registry » adds the repository missions, keeps one line per identifier and replaces a former identifier by the folder's; a line without a readable file stays (the file may not have arrived yet). An added mission takes its file's series, else the current series of its family (a number). In an identifier (« A-001A »), the final letter from A to Y is a flight linked to the mission without suffix, Z its organisation. « Correct the registry line » changes the title, series, number or operation of a line; « Remove from the registry » takes it out, the mission file stays in the repository. An unreadable registry is never rewritten; an absent one is created from the files. Nothing, in the registry or the Brief, says that a mission goes beyond its first objective; the box « See the full content of the missions », unticked each time Missions opens and never saved, makes the workshop open the whole mission. It is a secret of the screen: the mission file stays readable by whoever opens it.

<a id="missions-editor"></a>**The editor.** In the registry, « Create a mission » (at the head), « Workshop » and « Duplicate » (in the sheet of a line and its « ⋯ »; « Workshop » also in the Brief opened from the registry) open the editor, which has the shape of a workshop. A mission that exists opens veiled — you too may discover it by playing it: the workshop shows only what the pilot sees at the departure (« At the departure », its places, its route), saying nothing of what might follow; « Reveal the whole mission », in the unfolding, shows everything after a question (« Reveal » or « Keep the secret ») — this button is on every veiled mission, whether it hides something or not. Veiled, the mission is retouched (texts, positions, places added) and saved without losing anything hidden; removing an existing place or changing its identifier asks to reveal it. A mission you create is whole, a copy stays veiled. The workshop: the map of the mission (the suite's map, where the mission is drawn; touching a place chooses its row in the Places, « Recenter » brings the view back; offline, the map shows the places on a plain background and everything else works; under the map, « Background » chooses « Fast » or « Relief » (the topographic background) and « Airspaces » shows the R, D and P zones and the RTBA of the view, under the mission — this choice belongs to the mission: it is saved with it, and the pilot's map opens so; with no map at all, a sketch of the places), the unfolding, a list from top to bottom (« At the departure », then one drawer per row — its mark, its rank, its title and its key —, and « ＋ Drawer · Choose a brick », which opens the window « Choose a drawer brick » (full screen on a phone): a field « Search for a brick… », a line of counts, then groups to unfold — « Cross-cutting actions » first (precision landing, hover, back to one's aerodrome), one group per theme and, in it, a family and its bricks (each its symbol, its title and its key; its brief on hover), last « A key alone » (the pilot's gesture alone, an hour, a passage zone, an object to find, the arrival at the aerodrome…, without text); one touch creates the drawer with its key, its title and its message, closes the window, opens its sheet and says so in a notification whose « Undo » removes it; « Close » or Escape closes the window without creating anything; only the bricks whose key the flight can judge are offered, and the places they need stay to be placed — touching a row opens its sheet in place of the departure's: what opens the drawer (« Change the key » opens the same window: a key alone changes the key, a brick gives its key and its settings without touching your texts; a passage zone or an object to find are chosen among the Places; the pilot's gesture alone: he opens it by « Ask for what comes next »), its fallback time when the key looks at the aircraft (without a position received from the aircraft, the drawer opens at that time), « Show this zone to the pilot from the departure » (unticked by default: the passage zone stays hidden until the opening), its title — which the pilot reads once the drawer is open —, its message, its briefing, its onward route place by place, « What the pilot sees » — nothing while the drawer is closed (the pilot does not know it exists), then the open drawer —, and « Remove this drawer »; an arrival can require stops, added and removed one by one; « Try the keys », in the head of the unfolding, opens the trial in place of the sheet: the aircraft of the trial follows the route of the departure, you move the time on a slider, and the log says what opens each drawer (« zone entered », « object found », « landed at the arrival », the hour, the fallback time) or why it stays closed (« above its ceiling », « unknown — the altitude is missing »); the map shows the aircraft and its trail, each row says « open at H + 6 min » or « closed »; « Place the aircraft here », beside the line under the tools of the map, places the aircraft of the trial where you then touch the map, at the minute of the slider — a zone you place it in opens, an object within range is found; placing is not flying: a zone between the former position and the new one is not crossed; when it is within range of the object or in the zone of a drawer while an earlier drawer is closed, the log says so (« nothing opens, drawer 1 first »); « Start again » forgets the placements; it is the very computation of the flight, and the trial writes nothing in the mission; twelve drawers at most; a key the workshop cannot edit yet is kept as it is) and the sheet of the departure, what the pilot reads before leaving, in five groups (Frame, Teams, Story, Risks, Timeline and maps), under the frame « Places » at the head of the right column (on a phone, just under the map): its list of places, of fixed height, scrolls in itself; touching a row chooses the place, on the map too, and opens its fields under the list; touching a place on the map brings its row into view in the list. Each group says its content in one line; touching it unfolds it, touching it again folds it, one is unfolded at a time. On a phone the map unfolds by its button and a mission being edited opens with its groups folded; on a large screen the map and the unfolding are on the left, the sheet on the right. Under the map, « Profile » shows the terrain under the route of the part shown (the Map's vertical profile) and is redrawn when a place is moved; folded on a phone, open on a large screen; without terrain (offline, a refused tile) it says « Terrain unavailable » and draws the route without it, and with fewer than two places it asks you to place two; each place of the route is named under the axis, and when the terrain is read and a cruise level is written, a line says it when the cruise goes below the terrain (« The cruise goes below the terrain at 3.5 NM (625 ft). ») or keeps less than 500 ft of margin. At the foot, « Save » and « Cancel » side by side — « Cancel » leaves the workshop; if the mission carries an unsaved change, the workshop asks first: « Leave » or « Stay » (the words of the frame, which says the draft keeps everything: « Leave? Your changes stay as a draft on this device. »; the same when you leave the workshop by the menu, by the « Missions » tab or by the browser's « Back »: the workshop tells the frame, which asks and keeps it intact if you stay) — then « To complete (n) », which opens, just above, the named list of what is missing, never blocking. The map shows: departure and arrival in blue (for a liaison, their position comes from the aeronautical database), the target in red, the zones by their circle or their outline, the turning points, the rendezvous, the objects to find, and the route of the departure as a dashed line. Each place carries its identifier on it. Under the map, « View of the map » chooses what it shows: « Everything — the author's view », or the pilot's view, at the departure then after each drawer — the map then draws only what the pilot may see at that stage (a place cited by his route, by one of his maps or by a drawer already open; the zone of a closed drawer when you show it), and the tools come back with « Everything ». In a mission with drawers, a place, a zone or an object that nothing cites — neither the route, nor a map, nor a drawer — will never be shown to the pilot: « To complete » names it. The route of the departure is read as chips under the list of the Places: a cross removes a place, « Add a place… » adds one, and a place touched is chosen: the two arrows « ← Earlier » and « Later → » move it by one rank (in the onward route of a drawer too); in the route of the departure, the aerodrome of departure stays first and the aerodrome of arrival last — they are neither moved nor removed, and a place added enters before the arrival; on the map, the « Route » tool adds each place touched to the route of the departure — or, when a drawer is chosen in the unfolding, to its onward route — and stays in hand until it is taken again (« Undo the gesture » gives back the last place added this way). The map also shows, as small dots, the landmarks of the aeronautical database (aerodromes, beacons, fixes) when one gets closer: touching one says what it is, creating nothing, and « Add as a point » makes of it a turning point of the mission. Under the map, « Find a landmark » finds it by its code or its name: the map centres on it and circles it, as if you had touched it; when several landmarks answer, they are offered under the field. Above the map, the other tools: « Selection » (touching a place chooses it — it says its name, its line opens —, dragging it moves it; a round zone chosen shows the handle of its radius; the departure and the arrival are not moved), « Point », « Zone » and « Object » (a touch on the map poses the place there, then the tool goes back to « Selection »: nothing is created by accident); « Undo the gesture » gives back the places of before the last gesture on the map, never a typing, and « Redo » plays the last undone gesture again (any new gesture empties it). « Zone » poses the shape the tool shows, a circle or a polygon (an outline of six vertices): a second touch on the tool already chosen, or a long press, changes it (« Change to polygon », « Change to circle »). Every button of the map says its name on hover and to a screen reader. These buttons are icons of the suite (Selection, Point, Route, Zone — a circle or a polygon according to its shape —, Object, Undo the gesture, Redo, Recenter, and the Fast and Relief backgrounds); the tool in hand stays marked. A round zone becomes an outline by « Change to polygon », in its sheet: six vertices at its radius; chosen, it shows its vertices (they are dragged), between two vertices a point that adds one, and « Remove this vertex » for the vertex touched (three at least); its middle moves it as a block; the map frames it when its outline is drawn or when it is chosen (« Recenter » comes back to the mission); « Change to circle » gives its circle back. From the sheet of a drawer, « Draw on the map » (or « Pose on the map » for an object) poses the zone that becomes its key. The map background is asked of its provider (tiles.openfreemap.org), as for the Map: it receives the area shown, hence that of the mission; offline, nothing is asked. The Frame group (family and subtype, series, identifier, title, theatre, departure, arrival) and the Teams group (one row per team: callsign, type, service, role; « + Team » adds one, the cross removes it) and the Places group (one row per place: departure and arrival for their position, target, zones, turning points, rendezvous, with their position in degrees and minutes; touching it opens its fields, under the list, latitude and longitude in decimal degrees; « + Zone », « + Turning point », « + Rendezvous », « + Target », « + Object to find » (an object the pilot does not see while the drawer whose key it is stays closed: its kind, its position, its detection range, « found only when the pilot declares it »; its identifier is given by the workshop; its row, like the row of a zone, says the drawer that cites it: « key of drawer 2 »); a polygon keeps its points, still moved in the V2; renaming a place renames it wherever the mission cites it (the route, a drawer, a map, the timeline, the profile); « Remove » on a place still cited first says who cites it, then « Remove anyway » removes it with its citations, and « To complete » then says the drawer left without its key (its trigger point, its passage zone, the point of its arrival) or the map left without its centre) and the Story group (the summary, with its count of signs: the sheet shows it whole up to 600; the description, one paragraph per blank line; the main objective; the secondary objectives, one per line; the load, its mass and its passengers) and the Risks group (one row « hazard → mitigation » per risk — the sheet of the mission shows three on its first page; « Edit » opens the hazard, the consequence and the mitigation; « + Risk » adds one; a removed risk leaves its translations too) are filled there; what is typed is kept from group to group until « Save » or « Cancel ». The last one, Timeline and maps, gives the mission's events (one row per event: the time from the planned take-off, « H+30mn », a place of the mission or none, what happens; « + Event ») then the declared maps; « Propose maps from the places » adds the missing ones (general, target, rendezvous, zones), whose images are made in the V2 map studio. The organisation of an exercise (Z mission) stays in the V2. A commercial mission (family « E · CAT », a link flight) has a reduced Frame: departure, arrival, airline, load (passengers, cargo…) and aircraft; the title is proposed by itself (« LFPO → LFRS ») as long as you have not changed it; teams, risks, timeline and theatre are not asked. The family proposes the next identifier and the current series; only a missing family and an identifier without its form (« T-086 ») or already taken prevent saving, the rest is listed under « To complete ». « Save » writes the mission file and its registry line on the device: they go with the next send, and the editor says so (« changed on this device — not sent yet »). Only what you change is written, the rest of the file is kept as it is; « Save » without any change writes nothing. A file that uses YAML aliases (« &… », « *… ») does not open in the editor, and a registry that uses them is said unreadable and never rewritten: write the repeated object out in full at each place (or edit the mission in the V2); a changed identifier writes a new file, the former one is never deleted. A mission created without an airline is personal. Departure and Arrival are the aerodrome field of the suite, the one of Fuel: two letters propose the aerodromes of the base (seven at most, chosen with the keyboard or by touch), the field carries the code and the name is read under it; a place that is not an aerodrome is written as typed. A helistation (two letters and three digits) is chosen the same way. A turning point is also chosen among the points of the base (beacons, reporting points, network points): the Name field of its sheet (like Departure and Arrival) proposes them by their code and, once one is chosen, the place takes its position; free text stays as typed; with a landmark of the base chosen on the map, « + Turning point » and « + Target » add that landmark; a place name typed that is a code of the base gets its position. A named point of the base (aerodrome, helistation, beacon, reporting point), chosen or typed — in the departure and the arrival of the Cadre too —, writes its complete position in the place and locks it: the latitude and the longitude are read only, the sheet says « Position from the aeronautical base: the aerodrome LFPO — locked », and the map does not move it; the route of the departure and the profile go through it. To free it, erase its code: the place keeps its position, as a free point you can move. A known code typed or chosen on a place already placed moves it to the position from the base; a notification says so, and its « Undo » gives back its former position and name (a free name moves nothing, nor does a typed code that the base gives to several points: choose the right one in the list). In the Teams, a team's callsign completes as everywhere else: from two characters the callsigns of the known fleet are proposed with their type; choosing one writes the callsign and fills the line's ICAO type when only one type is known. A free text stays as typed, and nothing is written until you type or choose. The bricks of the window of « ＋ Drawer » are those of the suite's catalogue; the mission's family is unfolded first: a brick touched creates the drawer with its key, its title, its message and its fallback time, which you then change like those of any drawer (duration, places…, in its sheet). Under the title of the whole workshop, a line says the model of the mission (« Model: none » for a new mission); « Choose a model », then « Change model », open just below it the list of the models of the mission's family (choose the family in the Frame first): those of the suite (« Blank mission », « Search on a glacier », « Intercepting an aircraft ») then « Your models ». A model poses its drawers and its places « to place », without position, which you then place. If it must replace something, the workshop says so first: « Replaced » (the drawers of the mission, the model's places still to place) and « Kept » (the Frame, the Teams, the Story, the Risks, the Timeline, your places and the model's places you have already placed); « Keep the mission as it is » changes nothing. « Save as a model », on the same line (never in a veiled mission), asks for a name and files the mission among « Your models »: its complete drawers and its places, without any position, without its identifier, its teams or their callsigns; the model is written on the device and goes with the next sending. A model of the same name is replaced after a question (« Replace the model » or « Keep the old one »); the name of a model of the suite is refused. A text the reader refuses (a “<”, a “>” or an address) is not written in the model: the workshop says how many texts were left out. A saved model is not secret: anyone who opens the company’s data repository can read it. The workshop keeps the mission in progress as a draft, on this device only, at each change: nothing is published until you save, and « Leave » does not lose it. One draft per mission (a single one for a mission you create or copy), forty drafts at most (the oldest are erased); resuming it reopens the workshop as you left it, and “Save” makes it the mission and erases the draft. A drawer already written with a hover, a landing or an overflight (the flight judges the hover, the landing and the overflight, over a point or a still object, and « ＋ Drawer » offers them — the pilot of an overflight, once in range, touches “Ask for what comes next”; over a moving object the flight does not judge: the sheet says so, “opens by the gesture or at the fallback time”) shows their fields and can be changed: over a point or following an object of the mission, the radius (hover, overflight), the duration, the tolerated gap in fixes, the speed deviation, the maximum speed of a landing, the heights, the pilot's confirmation for an overflight, and its fallback time; a landing no longer asks for a radius: the sheet says “Judged by tiers: partial 0.5 NM · good 0.2 NM · perfect 0.1 NM · excellent 0.05 NM” (“Judged within 1 NM of the airfield” when it aims at the departure or the arrival), an arrival is judged within 1 NM of the airfield and a stop counts within 2 NM — a radius the file already carries is kept as it is; a value out of its bounds is not written, and “To complete” says what is missing (“condition to complete: Duration (s)”). The event is not offered under “＋ Drawer” as long as the flight does not judge it; the sequence of steps is, and the flight judges it when each of its steps is (no overflight to confirm, no confirmation word, no moving object); the tolerated gap in fixes of a landing or a hover is 5 s at least, and the heights (hover, overflight) are above the ground. A sequence of steps shows its steps in order, one open at a time: its kind (a hover, a landing or an overflight), its name, the fields of its rule and its confirmation word; the others fit on one line, touched to open it; “＋ Step” adds one, ten at most. A drawer awaiting a message from a crew says the identifier of that message and who sends it (“Sent by drawer 1 when it opens”); a drawer that sends one when it opens shows its identifier; its recipients are not typed (the workshop writes “all”, to every crew, until the ergonomist decides) and those a file already carries are kept as they are. The choice of the type offers only what the flight can judge; in the sheet of a drawer, its current key stays there, with its mention. The catalogue also carries bricks of hover, landing, overflight, sequence of steps and message: they appear under “＋ Drawer” only when the flight can judge their key. A drawer already written with a retardant refill (land at a retardant station, stop there, stay, leave again) is edited too: its sheet offers the station — only the “Retardant station” objects of the Places —, the minimum duration in minutes and, if you want, the docking heading, then says the rule in one line (“Land, stop within 30 m of the station, stay 5 min at least, then leave”); the flight does not judge it yet: it opens by the gesture or at the fallback time, and “＋ Drawer” does not offer it. A retardant station is not searched for: its sheet in the Places has no detection range, no “found only when the pilot declares it”, no heights.

## Good to know

- The module writes a mission only through its editor (« Create a mission », « Edit », « Duplicate »); the rest of the mission is still written in the CPCO.
- A mission whose file does not come says so: "not found" (the repository has not got it), "not on this device yet" (the synchronisation brings it, the mission appears when it arrives) or "unreadable" (with the reason). Only the registry is shown then.
- A mission that came with the synchronisation without the registry naming it is listed all the same, read from its file, at the next display; no button to press.
- A flight being prepared is replaced only after a question; a started flight never is (cancel the start, or finish the flight, first).
- The envelope, the page-by-page reader and the assignments will come later.
