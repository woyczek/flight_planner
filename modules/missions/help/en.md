# Missions

The airline's missions, in the Company application: those of the shared repository (written with the CPCO): choose one, or draw one at random, then prepare it in Fuel.

## How to use

<a id="missions-assigned"></a>**My assigned missions.** When the airline's centre assigns you a mission, it appears at the head of Missions, under "My assigned missions": the take-off (aerodrome, time Z), the aircraft, the callsign, and the crew's rendezvous and channels when the assignment carries them ("Open" for a link, "Copy" for a server or a frequency); "Prepare this flight" opens it in Fuel. The group exists only when an assignment carries your trigram; a cancelled assignment, or one whose take-off is more than 12 h past, is no longer there. Assignments are readable by every pilot of the airline: keep session passwords for the channel itself.

<a id="missions-list"></a>**The list.** The missions of the newest series first, grouped by family. Only the missions whose file is on this device are offered, in the list as in the draw; when there is none, the screen says "No readable mission on this device: synchronise". Each row gives the id, the title, the route, the aircraft type and the estimated block; "flown on …" marks a mission whose report you wrote. The search filters on the id, the title, an aerodrome (ICAO code), an aircraft type, a callsign (SAMU66) or the activity (MEDEVAC, HEMS, SAMU…), and also in the content of the missions already on the device: summary, description, objectives, risks, places, notes, callsigns and types, ignoring case, accents and hyphens ("clermont-ferrand" = "Clermont Ferrand"). A place that only a drawer cites is not searched, even once the drawer is open: the search never gives a closed drawer away. When the word is not in the title, the row says where it was found ("in: risks"); a mission whose file is not on the device yet is searched on its registry line, and the list says so ("n missions not read"). Nothing is written or downloaded to search. The registry search is the same; the drop-down list keeps one family, given by its letter (the one of the identifiers), its short name and its name ("T · AIRLIFT · Airlift / logistics"), in letter order; a chosen family with several subtypes opens a second one, "Subtype", beside it. Seven rows are shown, "N more" unfolds the rest. The organisation missions of an exercise (those ending in Z) are not flights and are not listed. Only the missions of the airline chosen in Settings › Airline are listed; a mission that names no airline is personal: it is under « Personnel ». The list « My airline · Personal · All airlines », above the search, also shows the personal missions or those of other airlines, each with the name of its airline; they can be prepared the same way. When the airline has categories (cargo, passengers…), a second drop-down keeps one category, and the open mission says its own.

<a id="missions-open"></a>**Choose, or draw at random.** A tap on a row opens the mission sheet, its Brief: its category, its line of choice (duration · aircraft · theatre, as in the registry), its summary in full under the title "Summary" (beyond 600 signs, "Read more" opens the detail), its risks (the first three, "hazard → mitigation", the others folded, or "No risk declared"), the teams engaged and its zones. "Detail" shows the rest in foldable sections: description (the detailed description only, without the summary), objectives, zones to activate, rendezvous, route provided, maps provided, payload and aircraft; "See on the Map" opens the Map on a zone or on a map of the mission (drawn from its coordinates). On a phone, the browser's back returns from the detail to the sheet, then to the list; on a large screen, the sheet and the detail are side by side. "Draw at random" picks one among the missions of the filtered list you have not flown yet; "Draw again" picks another. Nothing starts by itself: "Prepare this flight" opens Fuel, in the Pilot application, with the departure, the arrival, the points of the mission (as coordinates), the level and the aircraft type; the runway, the procedures, the fuel and the weather are then prepared as for any flight.

<a id="missions-drawers"></a>**The rest of the mission.** A mission may have a sequel that nothing announces: it is given to you in flight. On the sheet of YOUR mission — it is assigned to you, or it is the mission of your flight — it then appears under “Rest of the mission”: its title, how and when (“opened on entering the zone, at 20:10Z”), its message and its briefing; a notification says so. Nothing appears before. “Ask for what comes next”, next to “Prepare this flight”, is touched when you have reached or found what the mission asks for: the centre gives you what comes next if there is anything, otherwise it answers “Nothing new for now”; the command then rests for a minute. During the mission's flight, with a live position (this computer's simulator, the GPS, your online session), your device follows the flight without saying anything; without a live position, what comes next arrives at its time. What is given to a crew mate on their finding is given to you too — within the minute while your sheet is on screen, and the line says “received here at …” when it came later. At the Full level, “Reseal” closes again a part you opened, for you only. As long as a part is not given, the sheet and its Detail neither list nor name what it hides; a mission you are not playing shows only its visible part. The device keeps to itself what was given to you (nothing is sent): the Map uses it.

<a id="missions-add"></a>**Add missions.** « Add missions », next to « Draw at random », reads one or more YAML mission files (a file may hold several, separated by « --- » or in a `missions:` list), or a pasted YAML — or an assistant’s whole answer: its code blocks (« ```yaml … ``` », else « ``` … ``` ») are read, each as a source, and the text around them is ignored (the screen says so); an indentation with tabs is refused (YAML is indented with spaces) and an error is given with the line of the pasted text. Before writing, the screen shows what it read (id, title, family, series, airline) and what it refuses, with the reason (id outside the rule, exercise organisation, duplicate, YAML unreadable at a given line). « Add (n) » writes each mission at its place, `missions/<letter>_<FAMILY>/<ID>/mission<ID>.yaml`; a mission that already exists is replaced only when « replace? » is ticked. A mission without an airline is personal. They leave at the next synchronisation.

<a id="missions-airline"></a>**Change airline.** On a mission’s sheet, « Airline: … · Change » offers the airlines and « Personal »; the sentence « … moves from … to … » is said before « Write ». In the list, « Assign to an airline… » takes the missions shown (scope, family, search), each ticked: the screen says how many change and which are unreadable (left as they are) before writing. When adding from YAML, « Add these missions to » chooses their airline. Only the `airline` field of the file changes.

<a id="missions-registry"></a>**The registry.** « Registry », next to « Draw at random » (or the address `#missions?view=registry`), shows one line per mission as the registry `missions/mission_registry.yaml` writes it, with the state of its file: there, missing, unreadable or path refused; and the repository missions the registry does not know. As in the V2 table, a mission holds on one line: the sign of its state (✓ file there, ! to check, + not in the registry, … reading, ? not read: the screen reads 500 files at most and says so in the count), the identifier, the title, the family and the series, the origin and the destination, and below its line of choice « duration · aircraft · theatre »; a tap unfolds it: the state in words, the number, the operation, the path of the file, then « Brief » (the mission sheet as the pilot sees it, never the content of a closed drawer; « Back to the registry » returns to the same line), « Workshop », « Duplicate » and « ⋯ » (« Correct the registry line », « Remove from the registry »). The line of choice says what the mission file says; a missing duration is computed on the route given, at the cruise speed of the file or at a typical speed of the aircraft's class (helicopter, aeroplane), rounded to five minutes and marked « ≈ »; the theatre is the file's, else the State of the departure aerodrome; what is not known is not shown. The lines to check come first, then the others by series and identifier; the family filter (and its subtype, as in the list) and the search are at the head. « Update the registry » first says what it would do (missions to add, lines without a readable file, duplicate identifiers, former identifiers), then « Write the registry » adds the repository missions, keeps one line per identifier and replaces a former identifier by the folder's; a line without a readable file stays (the file may not have arrived yet). An added mission takes its file's series, else the current series of its family (a number). In an identifier (« A-001A »), the final letter from A to Y is a flight linked to the mission without suffix, Z its organisation. « Correct the registry line » changes the title, series, number or operation of a line; « Remove from the registry » takes it out, the mission file stays in the repository. An unreadable registry is never rewritten; an absent one is created from the files. Nothing, in the registry or the Brief, says that a mission goes beyond its first objective; the box « See the full content of the missions », unticked each time Missions opens and never saved, makes the workshop open the whole mission. It is a secret of the screen: the mission file stays readable by whoever opens it.

<a id="missions-editor"></a>**The editor.** In the registry, « Create a mission » (at the head), « Workshop » and « Duplicate » (on an unfolded line; « Workshop » also in the Brief opened from the registry) open the editor, which has the shape of a workshop: the map of the mission (the suite's map, where the mission is drawn; touching a place leads to its line of the Places, « Recenter » brings the view back; offline, the map shows the places on a plain background and everything else works; under the map, « Background » chooses « Fast » or « Relief » (the topographic background) and « Airspaces » shows the R, D and P zones and the RTBA of the view, under the mission — this choice belongs to the mission: it is saved with it, and the pilot's map opens so; with no map at all, a sketch of the places), the unfolding (« At the departure », then the drawers of the mission, said by their rank with their key, and « ＋ Drawer » — touching a card opens its sheet in place of the departure's: what opens the drawer (the pilot's gesture alone, an hour, a passage zone or an object to find chosen among the Places, the arrival at the aerodrome), its fallback time when the key looks at the aircraft, « Show this zone to the pilot from the departure » (unticked by default: the passage zone stays hidden until the opening), its title — which the pilot reads once the drawer is open —, its message, its briefing, its onward route place by place, « What the pilot sees » — nothing while the drawer is closed (the pilot does not know it exists), then the open drawer —, and « Remove this drawer »; an arrival can require stops, added and removed one by one; « Try the keys », in the head of the unfolding, opens the trial in place of the sheet: the aircraft of the trial follows the route of the departure, you move the time on a slider, and the log says what opens each drawer (« zone entered », « object found », « landed at the arrival », the hour, the fallback time) or why it stays closed (« above its ceiling », « unknown — the altitude is missing »); the map shows the aircraft and its trail, each card says « open at H + 6 min » or « closed »; « Place the aircraft here », beside the line under the tools of the map, places the aircraft of the trial where you then touch the map, at the minute of the slider — a zone you place it in opens, an object within range is found; placing is not flying: a zone between the former position and the new one is not crossed; when it is within range of the object or in the zone of a drawer while an earlier drawer is closed, the log says so (« nothing opens, drawer 1 first »); « Start again » forgets the placements; it is the very computation of the flight, and the trial writes nothing in the mission; twelve drawers at most; a key the workshop cannot edit yet is kept as it is) and the sheet of the departure, what the pilot reads before leaving, in six groups (Frame, Teams, Places, Story, Risks, Timeline and maps). Each group says its content in one line; touching it unfolds it, touching it again folds it, one is unfolded at a time. On a phone the map unfolds by its button and a mission being edited opens with its groups folded; on a large screen the map and the unfolding are on the left, the sheet on the right. At the foot, « Save » and « Cancel » side by side — « Cancel » leaves the workshop; if the mission carries an unsaved change, the workshop asks first: « Leave without saving » or « Stay in the workshop » — then « To complete (n) », which opens, just above, the named list of what is missing, never blocking. The map shows: departure and arrival in blue (for a liaison, their position comes from the aeronautical database), the target in red, the zones by their circle or their outline, the turning points, the rendezvous, the objects to find, and the route of the departure as a dashed line. Each place carries its identifier on it. Under the map, « View of the map » chooses what it shows: « Everything — the author's view », or the pilot's view, at the departure then after each drawer — the map then draws only what the pilot may see at that stage (a place cited by his route, by one of his maps or by a drawer already open; the zone of a closed drawer when you show it), and the tools come back with « Everything ». In a mission with drawers, a place, a zone or an object that nothing cites — neither the route, nor a map, nor a drawer — will never be shown to the pilot: « To complete » names it. The route of the departure is read as chips under the list of the Places: a cross removes a place, « Add a place… » adds one, and a place touched is chosen: the two arrows « ← Earlier » and « Later → » move it by one rank (in the onward route of a drawer too); in the route of the departure, the aerodrome of departure stays first and the aerodrome of arrival last — they are neither moved nor removed, and a place added enters before the arrival; on the map, the « Route » tool adds each place touched to the route of the departure — or, when a drawer is chosen in the unfolding, to its onward route — and stays in hand until it is taken again (« Undo the gesture » gives back the last place added this way). The map also shows, as small dots, the landmarks of the aeronautical database (aerodromes, beacons, fixes) when one gets closer: touching one says what it is, creating nothing, and « Add as a point » makes of it a turning point of the mission. Under the map, « Find a landmark » finds it by its code or its name: the map centres on it and circles it, as if you had touched it; when several landmarks answer, they are offered under the field. Above the map, the other tools: « Selection » (touching a place chooses it — it says its name, its line opens —, dragging it moves it; a round zone chosen shows the handle of its radius; the departure and the arrival are not moved), « Point », « Zone » and « Object » (a touch on the map poses the place there, then the tool goes back to « Selection »: nothing is created by accident); « Undo the gesture » gives back the places of before the last gesture on the map, never a typing. A round zone becomes an outline by « Draw its outline », in its sheet: six vertices at its radius; chosen, it shows its vertices (they are dragged), between two vertices a point that adds one, and « Remove this vertex » for the vertex touched (three at least); its middle moves it as a block; the map frames it when its outline is drawn or when it is chosen (« Recenter » comes back to the mission); « Make it round » gives its circle back. From the sheet of a drawer, « Draw on the map » (or « Pose on the map » for an object) poses the zone that becomes its key. The map background is asked of its provider (tiles.openfreemap.org), as for the Map: it receives the area shown, hence that of the mission; offline, nothing is asked. The Frame group (family and subtype, series, identifier, title, theatre, departure, arrival) and the Teams group (one row per team: callsign, type, service, role; « + Team » adds one, the cross removes it) and the Places group (one row per place: departure and arrival for their position, target, zones, turning points, rendezvous, with their position in degrees and minutes; « Edit » opens its fields, latitude and longitude in decimal degrees; « + Zone », « + Turning point », « + Rendezvous », « + Target », « + Object to find » (an object the pilot does not see while the drawer whose key it is stays closed: its kind, its position, its detection range, « found only when the pilot declares it »; its identifier is given by the workshop; its row, like the row of a zone, says the drawer that cites it: « key of drawer 2 »); a polygon keeps its points, still moved in the V2; renaming a place renames it wherever the mission cites it (the route, a drawer, a map, the timeline, the profile); « Remove » on a place still cited first says who cites it, then « Remove anyway » removes it with its citations, and « To complete » then says the drawer left without its key (its trigger point, its passage zone, the point of its arrival) or the map left without its centre) and the Story group (the summary, with its count of signs: the sheet shows it whole up to 600; the description, one paragraph per blank line; the main objective; the secondary objectives, one per line; the load, its mass and its passengers) and the Risks group (one row « hazard → mitigation » per risk; « Edit » opens the hazard, the consequence and the mitigation; « + Risk » adds one; a removed risk leaves its translations too) are filled there; what is typed is kept from group to group until « Save » or « Cancel ». The last one, Timeline and maps, gives the mission's events (one row per event: the time from the planned take-off, « H+30mn », a place of the mission or none, what happens; « + Event ») then the declared maps; « Propose maps from the places » adds the missing ones (general, target, rendezvous, zones), whose images are made in the V2 map studio. The organisation of an exercise (Z mission) stays in the V2. A commercial mission (family « E · CAT », a link flight) has a reduced Frame: departure, arrival, airline, load (passengers, cargo…) and aircraft; the title is proposed by itself (« LFPO → LFRS ») as long as you have not changed it; teams, risks, timeline and theatre are not asked. The family proposes the next identifier and the current series; only a missing family and an identifier without its form (« T-086 ») or already taken prevent saving, the rest is listed under « To complete ». « Save » writes the mission file and its registry line on the device: they go with the next send, and the editor says so (« changed on this device — not sent yet »). Only what you change is written, the rest of the file is kept as it is; « Save » without any change writes nothing. A file that uses YAML aliases (« &… », « *… ») does not open in the editor, and a registry that uses them is said unreadable and never rewritten: write the repeated object out in full at each place (or edit the mission in the V2); a changed identifier writes a new file, the former one is never deleted. A mission created without an airline is personal.

## Good to know

- The module writes a mission only through its editor (« Create a mission », « Edit », « Duplicate »); the rest of the mission is still written in the CPCO.
- A mission whose file does not come says so: "not found" (the repository has not got it), "not on this device yet" (the synchronisation brings it, the mission appears when it arrives) or "unreadable" (with the reason). Only the registry is shown then.
- A mission that came with the synchronisation without the registry naming it is listed all the same, read from its file, at the next display; no button to press.
- A flight being prepared is replaced only after a question; a started flight never is (cancel the start, or finish the flight, first).
- The envelope, the page-by-page reader and the assignments will come later.

- A closed drawer is not a secret: its content is in the mission file, which every pilot can read. It is a game's seal. Without accounts, a pilot of the mission who writes a false finding in their record opens the drawer for the others: the screen always says by whom, when and how a drawer was opened.
